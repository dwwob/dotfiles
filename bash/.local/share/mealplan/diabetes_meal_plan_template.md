# 🩺 Type 2 Diabetes Remission Plan & Logs

## 🥗 3-Day Glucose Stabilization Plan

### Example Meal Plan

### Day 1

- **Breakfast:** 3 scrambled eggs in butter/olive oil with a large handful of fresh spinach; 1/2 fresh avocado.
- **Lunch:** Grilled chicken salad over dark leafy greens dressed with extra virgin olive oil, lemon juice, and 1 tbsp crushed walnuts.
- **Dinner:** Thick-cut pan-seared pork chop or steak (rare), served with a generous side of roasted broccoli florets in garlic oil.
- **Movement:** 15-minute brisk walk immediately after dinner.

### Day 2

- **Breakfast:** 3-egg omelet stuffed with sautéed mushrooms and a small sprinkle of cheese.
- **Lunch:** Turkey and avocado lettuce wraps (6-8 oz sliced turkey breast wrapped in romaine leaves with avocado and cucumbers).
- **Dinner:** Baked salmon or white fish fillet served over a bed of riced cauliflower sautéed in olive oil and garlic.
- **Movement:** 15-minute brisk walk immediately after dinner.

### Day 3

- **Breakfast:** 3 hard-boiled eggs sliced over a bed of sautéed spinach and mushrooms.
- **Lunch:** Tuna salad bowl (canned tuna mixed with olive-oil mayo, celery bits, and black pepper) over large bowl of dark greens.
- **Dinner:** Slow-cooked beef chuck roast or short ribs with a side of roasted asparagus spears tossed in olive oil. (No potatoes/carrots).
- **Movement:** 15-minute brisk walk immediately after dinner.

## 📋 Questions to Bring to Your Doctor

1. **Metformin Effectiveness:** Given that my HbA1c is currently at 10% after 6 years of diagnosis, is my current dose of Metformin still sufficient, or do we need to temporarily adjust it to bring my numbers down safely?
2. **Addressing Symptoms:** I am experiencing frequent urination right now. Are my kidneys spilling glucose because of the 10% baseline, and what is our target blood sugar range to stop this symptom?
3. **Dietary Adjustments:** I am starting an intensive low-carbohydrate, high-fiber dietary plan to lower my blood sugar naturally. Do I need to monitor for low blood sugar (hypoglycemia), or does Metformin remain safe to take alongside a low-carb routine?
4. **OTC Monitoring:** I plan to start using an over-the-counter Continuous Glucose Monitor (like a Dexcom Stelo). What daily fasting and post-meal glucose numbers should I be aiming for on the log?
